Have a question? We’re here to answer.

What is an ALS certification, and why is it important for my medical career?

ALS (Advanced Life Support) certification equips you with the essential skills to respond to cardiac and other critical emergencies. This certification is highly valued in healthcare systems globally, especially in the UK and Australia, and is often a requirement or preferred qualification for roles in emergency and critical care.

Is this ALS course recognized internationally?

Yes. This is an ERC-accredited course — India’s first ALS course recognised in the UK and Australia. The certificate is valid for up to 3 years and is respected by healthcare systems internationally.

What kind of practical skills will I learn in this course?

You will train in the ABCDE approach to cardiac arrest, CPR, airway management, recognising patients at risk of deterioration, and the non-technical skills needed to lead and collaborate with a resuscitation team under pressure — all through high-fidelity simulation.

Who can take this ALS course, and are there any prerequisites?

The course is designed for doctors and healthcare professionals, including those preparing for PLAB-2 and international medical practice. Contact us to confirm eligibility for your specific role and stage.

How is this course structured, and what can I expect during the training?

It is a comprehensive 2-day course combining ERC-accredited curriculum content with realistic simulation-based learning, instant feedback and expert coaching, continuous assessment in airway management and emergency response, plus MCQ exams and CASTests.

What resources or materials will I receive as part of the ALS course?

You receive access to essential ALS study materials alongside hands-on training with real-life simulations, delivered by ERC-certified instructors.
